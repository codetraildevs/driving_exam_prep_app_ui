# Privacy Policy — DrivePrep Rwanda

**Last updated:** March 2026

---

## 1. Introduction

DrivePrep Rwanda ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data.

This policy applies to the **DrivePrep Rwanda** mobile application (the "App"), available on Android.

---

## 2. Data We Collect

### 2.1 Account Information
When you register, we collect:
- **Full name** — to personalise your experience
- **Phone number** — used as your unique login credential

### 2.2 Device Identifier
We collect a **SHA-256 hashed Android ID** (a device fingerprint). This is a one-way hash of your device's `Settings.Secure.ANDROID_ID`. It cannot be reversed to reveal the original identifier. We use this to:
- Secure your account against unauthorized access
- Detect and prevent fraudulent activity

### 2.3 Exam Results & Progress
We store your practice exam scores, quiz attempts, and learning progress so you can track your improvement over time.

### 2.4 Subscription Data
If you subscribe to premium features, we record your subscription tier and activation date. Payment is processed externally via **MTN MoMo Pay** — we do **not** store or have access to your Mobile Money PIN or financial account details.

### 2.5 Offline Cache
The App caches exam data and your results locally on your device using SharedPreferences so you can study without an internet connection. This data remains on your device and is synced to our server when connectivity is restored.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Data

| Purpose | Data Used |
|---|---|
| Account authentication | Phone number, name |
| Account security | Hashed device ID |
| Learning analytics | Exam results, progress |
| Offline functionality | Cached exam data |
| Subscription management | Subscription tier, activation date |

We do **not** use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the App's educational function.

---

## 4. Data Storage & Security

- All data is transmitted over **HTTPS** (TLS 1.2+). The App does not permit cleartext HTTP traffic.
- Personal data is stored on secured servers.
- The device identifier is stored only as a **SHA-256 hash** — never in plaintext.
- We employ industry-standard security measures including input validation, parameterised database queries, and access controls.

---

## 5. Data Sharing

We do **not** sell, trade, rent, or share your personal data with any third party. Specifically:
- No third-party analytics SDKs are embedded in the App
- No advertising networks receive your data
- No data is shared with government bodies, examination authorities, or driving schools

Data may only be disclosed if required by applicable law or a valid court order.

---

## 6. Data Retention

- Your data is retained for as long as your account is active.
- If you delete your account (via Profile → Delete Account), all personal data is permanently erased from our servers within 30 days.
- Locally cached data is removed when the App is uninstalled.

---

## 7. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access** your personal data at any time through the App (Profile section)
- **Correct** your name or phone number through your profile settings
- **Delete** your account and all associated data
- **Withdraw consent** by deleting your account
- **Data portability** — contact us to request a copy of your data

To exercise these rights, use the in-app options or contact us at the address below.

---

## 8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for driving theory exam preparation. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

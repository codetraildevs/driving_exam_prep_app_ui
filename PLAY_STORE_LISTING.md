## Full Description (max 4000 characters)

```
Prepare for your Rwanda driving theory exam with confidence! DrivePrep Rwanda is the ultimate study companion for anyone learning traffic rules, road signs, and driving theory in Rwanda.

📚 PRACTICE EXAM-STYLE QUESTIONS
Work through hundreds of multiple-choice questions inspired by Rwandan driving exam topics and supplemented with expert-prepared material. Each question covers traffic rules, road signs, right-of-way, speed limits, and safe driving practices.

📊 TRACK YOUR PROGRESS
Monitor your learning journey with detailed statistics. See your scores improve over time, identify weak areas, and know exactly when you're ready for the real exam.

🌍 THREE LANGUAGES
Study in the language you're most comfortable with — English, French, or Kinyarwanda. Switch languages anytime from the settings menu.

✅ KEY FEATURES
• Hundreds of practice questions covering all exam topics
• Timed practice exams simulating real test conditions
• Detailed progress tracking and performance analytics
• Road signs reference with clear explanations
• Works offline after initial setup
• Clean, modern interface optimized for all screen sizes
• Dark mode support for comfortable studying at night
• Regular content updates

🎯 WHO IS THIS FOR?
• First-time driving license applicants in Rwanda
• Anyone renewing or upgrading their driving category
• Driving school students looking for extra practice
• Anyone wanting to refresh their knowledge of Rwandan traffic rules

⚠️ DISCLAIMER
DrivePrep Rwanda is an independent educational application designed to help you study and practice for the driving theory exam. This app is NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any government authority, examination body, or driving school. All content is for educational and practice purposes only and does not guarantee exam results.

Start practicing today and pass your driving theory exam with confidence!
```
